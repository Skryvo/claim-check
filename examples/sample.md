# The sea and a statue

The ocean covers about 71 percent of the Earth ([NOAA](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/howmanyoceans.html)).

The Statue of Liberty was dedicated in 1896 ([National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/stli/index.htm)).

The Pacific is the largest ocean on Earth.[^pacific]

About 70% of travellers say a famous landmark is the main reason they choose a city.

New York is the most beautiful city to visit in spring.

[^pacific]: https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/pacific.html
